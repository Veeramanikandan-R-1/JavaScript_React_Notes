* range update average

* Container With Most Water with two pointers

## Interview Patterns

* Recursion: nested object flattening and department salary.
* Map/frequency: top K and keyboard rows.
* Stack: removable adjacent letters.
* Stack: valid parentheses.
* Two pointers: Container With Most Water.
* Async workers: promise pool with max concurrency.
* Difference array: efficient range updates.
* Frequency map: single-occurrence values.
